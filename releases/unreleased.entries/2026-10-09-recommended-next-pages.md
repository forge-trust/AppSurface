### AppSurface Docs recommended next reads

AppSurface Docs keeps authored related links first and can add up to three explainable, metadata-backed next reads. Authors can disable suggestions independently of `related_pages`, and search-index clients receive complete rows with projected paths, source, and reason codes. Existing exact release archives remain unchanged until intentionally re-exported. Start with the [three-page authoring walkthrough](../../Web/ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Docs/use-appsurface-docs.md#build-a-three-page-next-read-example) and the [policy and JSON reference](../../Web/ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Docs/README.md#recommended-next-reads).

New exports also preserve working publish-root HTML URLs and bind logical page identities to their actual ordinary HTML files for archive alias recovery. Upgrade Docs and the export CLI together and intentionally re-export to adopt the [frozen artifact route contract](../../Web/ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Docs/README.md#frozen-archive-artifact-routes); historical v1 archives keep their recorded behavior.
