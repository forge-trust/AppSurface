- [Aspire testing hooks](../../Aspire/ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Aspire.Testing/README.md#configured-graph-only-proof) let tests supply activation configuration and services before profile constructors run, then configure the same Aspire graph before its components are composed. Use `CreateConfiguredAsync` with `AppSurfaceAspireTestingOptions` for per-call scenarios; existing `CreateAsync` calls keep their current behavior. Hooks are synchronous, activation and Aspire providers stay separate, and the factory returns a composed builder without building or starting the application.
