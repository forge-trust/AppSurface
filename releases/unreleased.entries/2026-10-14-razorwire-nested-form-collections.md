- [RazorWire form interactions](../../Web/ForgeTrust.RazorWire/Docs/form-interactions.md#nested-collections-and-index-tokens) can add, duplicate, and remove nested model-bound rows while keeping each child collection scoped to its owning row. Nested templates use distinct ancestor and child index tokens; existing one-level templates retain the `__index__` default. Parent duplication allocates fresh descendant keys, clears recognized hidden identities, and omits descendants already marked for deletion. Start with the [MVC nested-collections quickstart](../../examples/razorwire-mvc/NESTED-COLLECTIONS.md) for DateOnly fields, physical and mark removal, sparse-key validation redisplay, and ordinary form commands without JavaScript. Applications still own validation, persistence, deletion policy, and server commands. Follow the [migration guidance](../../Web/ForgeTrust.RazorWire/Docs/form-interactions.md#migrating-an-existing-one-level-collection) to update the package and matching browser assets together and retain posted keys, attempted values, and errors after an invalid submission. The linked V4 fixture provides local, version-bound consumer proof; live Skoolit adoption and human first-use timing remain unverified.
