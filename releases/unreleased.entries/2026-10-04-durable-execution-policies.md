### Durable execution policies — [issue #765](https://github.com/forge-trust/AppSurface/issues/765)

Durable Work can retain legacy completion-relative backoff or opt into an exclusive absolute deadline and deterministic acceptance-relative retry slots. PostgreSQL makes each effect admission decision against the authoritative cutoff; cancellation of an already-running executor remains cooperative. Schema `12` raises the compatible reader/writer floor, so stop new submissions and drain pre-schema-12 hosts and activators before applying the migration. Deploy compatible packages to every host before accepting the new Work version. For rollback, stop new acceptance and keep schema-12-capable packages available to drain or reconcile accepted Work; do not restore a pre-floor binary. Follow the canonical [execution-policy guide](https://github.com/forge-trust/AppSurface/blob/codex/make-it-so-765-retry-deadlines/Durable/execution-policies-v1.md) and [schema-12 migration and rollback checklist](https://github.com/forge-trust/AppSurface/blob/codex/make-it-so-765-retry-deadlines/Durable/migrations/execution-policies-v1.md).
