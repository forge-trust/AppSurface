### Immediate feedback for RazorWire forms

- [RazorWire](../../Web/ForgeTrust.RazorWire/README.md) now shows an app-authored status as soon as an enhanced form submission is accepted, including waits for lazy anti-forgery preparation or a slow server. Forms without an applicable status use a compact top bar by default. The [form loading guide](../../Web/ForgeTrust.RazorWire/Docs/form-loading.md) explains indicator boundaries, styling, opt-outs, duplicate submission protection, and retry behavior after token failure.
