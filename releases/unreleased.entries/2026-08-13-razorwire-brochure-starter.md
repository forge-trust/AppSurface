### RazorWire brochure starter

The [RazorWire brochure starter](../../examples/razorwire-brochure-starter/README.md) adds a package-only MVC field-notes site for teams evaluating a small, exportable RazorWire application. It uses only `ForgeTrust.RazorWire`, makes its demo contact behavior explicit, and exports six CDN-ready pages without a message-delivery endpoint.
