### Policy-driven CI evidence pilot

AppSurface maintainers can rehearse a revision-bound [EvidenceHost plan](../../start-here/evidencehost.md) from a protected policy, an exact base/head diff, and a credentialless subject job. The [rollout record](../../docs/evidence-gate-rollout.md) explains the current non-claiming workflow, its image provenance and cleanup checks, and the live PR cases still needed before the evidence check can become required. This pilot intentionally fails closed; it does not yet authorize a merge or a release.
