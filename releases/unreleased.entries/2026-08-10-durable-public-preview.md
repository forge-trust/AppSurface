### Durable package availability

- The next coordinated prerelease publishes [`ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Durable`](https://github.com/forge-trust/AppSurface/blob/main/Durable/ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Durable/README.md), [`ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Durable.Provider`](https://github.com/forge-trust/AppSurface/blob/main/Durable/ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Durable.Provider/README.md), and [`ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Durable.PostgreSql`](https://github.com/forge-trust/AppSurface/blob/main/Durable/ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Durable.PostgreSql/README.md) as public previews. Start with the [package chooser](https://github.com/forge-trust/AppSurface/blob/main/packages/README.md), then follow the PostgreSQL [schema and role deployment path](https://github.com/forge-trust/AppSurface/blob/main/Durable/ForgeTrust.AppSurface.Durable.PostgreSql/README.md#explicit-schema-and-epoch-deployment) before enabling the explicit worker host. The packages retain their preview boundaries: no automatic DDL or worker activation, public endpoints, exactly-once external effects, or production support. The first supported publication and any persisted deployment independently freeze command canonical bytes; after either, a change requires a new fingerprint schema id.
